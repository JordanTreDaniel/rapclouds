01 — Quick Explainer
What Is A2P 10DLC?

A2P (Application-to-Person) SMS is any text sent from software — like Twilio, your CRM, or a dialer — not from a personal phone. 10DLC (10-Digit Long Code) is the standard local phone number format used for this traffic. Since August 31, 2023, all major carriers block unregistered A2P traffic. If Resource Realty's numbers aren't registered, every text you send gets dropped silently — or worse, flagged for spam and blacklisted.

03 — The 5 Rules That Matter
Compliance Non-Negotiables
1
Opt-Out Language
Every first message must include "Reply STOP to opt out". Honor STOP and all variants within 10 business days. No exceptions.
2
Quiet Hours
No texts before 8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM in the recipient's timezone — not yours.
3
Consent Documentation
Must be documented with: timestamp, method, phone number, identity, and campaign name.
4
National DNC Registry
The National Do Not Call Registry applies to SMS per 2025–2026 court rulings. Check it.
5
Keep Records
Retain consent records with timestamps, source, and campaign for at least 4 years.
04 — How to Get Consent (Legally)
Building Your Opt-In List
These are the practical, legal ways to earn texting permission. Each one gives you documented consent.
📝
Web Form Checkbox
Unchecked by default, voluntary. Lead fills out your form → opted in → can text.
💬
"Text Me" on a Call
They say "text me" during a conversation → express consent → can text immediately.
📧
Email Response
They reply to your email → express consent. Document the email thread as proof.
🔑
SMS Keyword Opt-In
They text a keyword to your number → express written consent. Best practice: double opt-in.
✍️
Signed Written Consent
A physical or digital form they sign. Strongest possible consent for audit defense.
📱
Double Opt-In
They opt in → you send a confirmation text → they confirm. Gold standard for compliance.

10 — Campaign Vetting Tips
Getting Your Campaign Approved
TCR vetting is strict. One mistake = rejection and 2–3 week delay. Follow these rules.
✓ Include
  • Legal business name matching EIN exactly
  • Message samples that match what you'll actually send
  • Use "Message and data rates may apply" verbatim
  • Privacy Policy URL (required after June 30, 2026)
  • Terms & Conditions URL (required after June 30, 2026)
  • Clear opt-in/opt-out process description
  • Accurate use case: "client follow-up" or "inquiry responses"
✕ Exclude / Never Say
  • Never describe cold outreach as campaign purpose — TCR rejects it
  • Don't bundle opt-ins across multiple campaigns
  • Don't use generic or vague message samples
  • Don't skip the Privacy Policy or T&C URLs
  • Don't use a different business name than your EIN record
  • Don't claim opt-in methods you don't actually use
