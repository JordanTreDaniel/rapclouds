Bugs Fixed from v3

  • mode='RGB' default — broke occupancy detection (alpha=255 everywhere → 100% occupancy → no room for other layers)
  • Dark pixel thresholding order — rasterizing occupancy after thresholding made dark words invisible to collision detection
  • wc.words_ ≠ placed count — was reporting vocab size instead of actual words on canvas
