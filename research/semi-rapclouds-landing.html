How it works

Three steps from idea to wearable art.

1

Pick your music

Tell us the artist, album, or specific songs. Any genre, any language. The more lyrics, the richer the cloud.

2

Choose your look

Pick a color palette (greens, reds, blues, custom hex), a mask shape (face, silhouette, portrait), and a font style.

3

We make it

Each piece is generated at high resolution, optimized for print. You get a preview before anything ships.
